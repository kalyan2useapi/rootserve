Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Rootserve ("we", "us") collects through www.rootserve.us, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question about it, email hello@rootserve.us.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in our contact form, email us, call, text or message us on WhatsApp, we receive what you choose to share. On the form that is your name, work email, company, company size, your role, what you would like AI to help with, your preferred way to be contacted and, if you add it, a phone or WhatsApp number.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when pages load, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate, for example which pages are viewed, how people arrive and which buttons they click. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology and processes this data on our behalf under Google's terms. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, schedule and prepare for your AI Assessment, and follow up on the conversation you started.
  • To provide, run and improve our services and this website.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam or misuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not send marketing newsletters unless you ask for them, and you can tell us to stop contacting you at any time.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business and only for that purpose. These include Zoho (our contact form and email), DigitalOcean and its content delivery network (website hosting), Google (analytics), and WhatsApp if you contact us there. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and to manage any relationship that follows, and generally no longer than three years after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email hello@rootserve.us and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, for example California, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them. We will not treat you differently for using them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on the tools that hold your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card numbers or health records through the contact form.

Children

Our services are for businesses. This site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted on the site.
