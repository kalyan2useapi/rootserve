What does Rootserve do?

Rootserve is an AI consulting and engineering firm based in Miami. We help small and mid-sized companies find where AI pays back, then design, build and run the solution: AI strategy, custom AI agents and copilots, workflow automation, data and predictive analytics, AI governance and managed AI operations.

What is an AI agent, and what can it do for a small business?

An AI agent is software that handles a defined job from start to finish, such as answering customer inquiries, scheduling appointments, reading invoices or following up on leads. It works inside the tools you already use, like your calendar, CRM, inbox or accounting system, and it asks a person for approval whenever your rules say it should.

How long does it take to see results?

The assessment takes about two weeks and ends with a ranked list of use cases. The first pilot runs for 30 to 45 days with real users and real data. Success metrics are agreed before we build anything, so you know early whether it is working.

Do we need our own data team or technical staff?

No. We handle the design, build and integration, connect to the systems you already run, and train your team to use what we deliver. If you want ongoing support, our managed AI operations plan covers monitoring, tuning and a monthly value report.

How do you keep our data and customers safe?

Before we build, we agree on what data each AI tool can see and where it is stored. We keep people in the loop for decisions that need judgment, log what agents do, and put a written AI use policy in place so your team knows the rules.

What does the free AI Assessment call include?

It is a 30-minute call with a senior practitioner. We review how your business runs and leave you with your top three AI opportunities and a rough sense of the payback for each. There is no cost and no obligation.

Do you only work with businesses in Miami?

We are based in Miami and work with companies across the United States. Most of the work happens remotely, and we meet in person in South Florida when that helps.

How much does an AI project cost?

It depends on scope. The assessment call is free, and every engagement after that has a fixed scope and agreed success metrics before any build starts, so you see the cost and expected return up front.
